What to expect

Deal with what you have been carrying

Most men are never given a place to put down what they carry, or a moment that marks the change from who they were taught to be to who they are. This is built to be that place.

You will work alone, outdoors, and in a small group of men who are there for the same reason. The work is direct and it can be intense. You decide how far you go, and nobody is pushed past his own line.

  1. 01

    Shadow work and self-inquiry

    Writing and reflection that bring up the parts of yourself you usually keep out of view.

  2. 02

    Nature-based reflection

    Quiet time on the land, alone with what came up.

  3. 03

    Drumming and movement

    The group comes back together through rhythm.

  4. 04

    Experiential group work

    The center of the work. Each man who chooses to gets his own turn to work something through, physically and out loud, with the group holding it.

  5. 05

    Connection

    You witness other men's work and they witness yours. We close around a fire.

Our intentions

  • Explore your authenticity
  • Be seen, heard and supported
  • Honor your own boundaries
  • Deepen trust in yourself

When was the last time you felt truly seen?

Men are often taught to be strong, keep it together, figure things out on their own, and carry the weight of their responsibilities without asking for help.

Over time, we learn to disconnect from parts of ourselves. We bury our anger, hide our fears, avoid vulnerability, and develop patterns that shape how we show up in our relationships and in the world. Somewhere along the way, we can lose connection with who we really are.

We believe men need spaces where they can be honest about what they're carrying, express themselves, question the expectations they've inherited, and find real connection and support from other men.

Men's Rite of Passage is an invitation to explore what it means to be fully yourself, in the company of other men willing to do the same.

Why a rite of passage

You get to decide what being a man means to you.

Throughout history, communities have created rituals and shared experiences to mark important transitions in life. For many men today, those chances for reflection, connection and recognition are missing.

This is a chance to examine the stories you've inherited about masculinity, the patterns you've developed to protect yourself, and the kind of man you want to become.

What to expect

Go into the hard parts, then come back together

This is an immersive, experiential gathering. You engage with yourself and with the group through a mix of individual and group processes. Most of it happens indoors, with time outside when the weather allows.

The work is direct and it can be intense. You decide how far you go, and nobody is pushed past his own line.

What comes after the emotional work matters just as much. The group comes back together for connection, support and integration before anyone goes home.

  1. 01

    Emotional integrity

    Explore the relationship between your intentions, your actions, and the fears that sometimes keep you stuck: how your beliefs and protective patterns shape the choices you make.

  2. 02

    Authentic connection and vulnerability

    Facilitated group conversations and exercises where you practice emotional honesty, say what you're experiencing, and connect with other men in a real way.

  3. 03

    Nature-based reflection

    Time outdoors in guided reflection, weather permitting, exploring your relationship with yourself and the natural world.

  4. 04

    Self-inquiry and reflective writing

    Slow down, examine the stories you've carried, and connect with parts of yourself that may have been overlooked or pushed aside.

  5. 05

    Experiential carpet work

    Facilitated emotional work that helps you recognize and express feelings, look at your patterns, and receive support from the group. It can bring up intense emotions and may include physical interaction, always optional: any touch happens only with your explicit consent.

  6. 06

    Drumming, movement and celebration

    After the emotional work, the group comes back together through expression, movement, music and shared celebration: connection, joy, and the freedom of being fully human.

  7. 07

    Dinner, closing and integration

    We share a simple dinner, then end with reflection and conversation about what you experienced, what you're taking with you, and how to carry it into your everyday life.

Our intentions

At the heart of this is a belief that healing happens in relationship.

  • Explore your authenticity
  • Be seen, heard and supported
  • Honor your own boundaries
  • Deepen trust in yourself
  • Connect through shared humanity

Our work is grounded in consent, autonomy, emotional integrity and mutual respect.

Who is this for?

Men who are curious about themselves and want a deeper relationship with their emotional lives.

Maybe you've spent years holding everything together for everyone else.

Maybe you struggle to express what you're feeling, find yourself repeating the same patterns in relationships, or feel disconnected from other men.

Maybe you're going through a transition, or questioning what masculinity, strength and authenticity mean to you.

Or maybe you're simply ready for something different.

You don't need any experience with shadow work, men's groups or experiential workshops. Bring your curiosity, your questions, and a willingness to take part in a way that feels right for you.

Questions

Is this therapy?

This is an experiential personal growth workshop focused on emotional awareness, self-exploration and authentic connection. It is not group therapy or a substitute for mental health treatment.

How does payment work?

The suggested total is $250. You pay the $100 reservation now to confirm your spot, and that is all you owe. After the event we email you a link for a second payment. It is completely optional and you set the amount. We suggest $150 or more, which brings you to the suggested total, and you can pay less, more, or nothing.

What if I register and cannot come?

The $100 reservation is nonrefundable. If you cancel at least 7 days before (by Saturday, November 28), we can offer a transfer or a credit toward a future event, subject to availability. Later cancellations are not guaranteed a credit.

Do I have to take part in everything?

No. You choose how much you share, you can decline or stop any exercise, and you can ask for support at any point. Any touch happens only with your explicit consent. Before the event you sign a participation agreement that covers this, along with confidentiality.

What if something difficult comes up?

Meaningful emotional work can bring up difficult experiences. We make space for grounding, reflection and integration throughout, and you can step back whenever you need to.

Is it substance-free?

Yes. Please arrive sober and don't use alcohol or other intoxicating substances during the event. Prescribed medications are fine as directed.

Who do I ask about anything else?

Email christie@prismaticrecovery.com.
